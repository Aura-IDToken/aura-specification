## 1. What this package is, and is not

**Is:** a normalization pass over the existing BC-02 / BC-02.1 governance
records, and a single routing point for everything they leave unresolved.

**Is not:** an amendment. No existing record is edited, restated, superseded by
fiat, or re-scored. Where two records diverge, both are quoted and the
divergence is named; **the divergence is not settled here.** Section 3's
normalized vocabulary is a *reading aid for the corpus*, not a rewrite of any
record's own words.

**Not done:** BC-02, BC-02.1 v1, BC-02.1 v2, APS-200, CONF-003, P-01, the
receiver implementations and the existing receipts are untouched. No Registry,
issuance record or fixture was created. B-VAL-012, B-VAL-014, CONF-003 §4.5,
BNC-1…BNC-7, N-01…N-08 and P-01-as-conformance were not executed. §7 records
the full firewall.

## 2. How Decision A and Decision B were derived

The terms *Decision A*, *Decision B*, *Decision Package* and *Terrain Survey*
occur in **no reachable commit of any in-scope repository**; they are this
work package's framing, not corpus vocabulary. The two decisions were therefore
derived, not inherited, and the derivation is stated so the Custodian can
correct it.

Every unresolved item in the corpus was collected (§6) and partitioned by the
question it turns on:

```text
Does an authority exist that can make P-01 an issued fixture?        →  DECISION A
What do B-VAL-014's operands mean, and can BNC-1 be caught?          →  DECISION B
```

The partition is exhaustive and disjoint over the corpus: every item in §6 falls
under exactly one track, or is marked execution-gated and therefore not a
decision at all. **A governs PRE-03; B governs PRE-02.** If the Custodian
intended a different partition, §6's routing table is the part to re-cut; §3's
normalization stands independently of it.

## 5. DECISION B — Operand semantics and BNC-1 detectability

> **What do B-VAL-014's two operands denote; and under that reading, can BNC-1
> be caught by B-VAL-012 and B-VAL-014 as §15.3 requires?**

**NOT RESOLVED HERE.**

### 5.1 The finding the decision rests on

BC-02 §8 states what the adapter receives: *"1. `input_segment_bytes` — an
octet buffer."* The §6.3 decode is therefore issuer-side, and the receiver is
handed the segment already decoded.

BC-02 §7.1 then defines both operands over receiver-side material:

```text
raw_input_reference.input_segment_sha256   "hex SHA-256 of decoded octets
                                            — RECOMPUTED BY THIS RECEIVER"
receipt.receipt_digest                     "hex SHA-256 recomputed over the
                                            buffer actually received"
```

If the buffer actually received **is** the decoded segment, both operands are
SHA-256 over one buffer on one side. Applying BNC-1 — *"flip one octet of the
buffer delivered to one side only"* — the flipped side records `H(corrupted)`
in both operands, so B-VAL-014's `receipt_digest == input_segment_sha256`
**still holds there**, and only B-VAL-012's cross-receiver conjunct diverges.

A second reading exists in the same contract: §5.3 says the value is *"computed
once by the issuer at seal time"*, and §7.1 carries `matches_issued_digest`.
If the record's `input_segment_sha256` is the **issuer-sealed** value echoed in,
B-VAL-014 compares receiver recomputation against issuer seal and catches BNC-1
exactly as §15.3 requires — but that contradicts §7.1's own inline
*"RECOMPUTED BY THIS RECEIVER"*.

**BC-02 §5.3, §7.1, §14 and §15.3 are not jointly satisfiable without a
determination of which value that field carries.** Reported, not resolved.

| ID | Question | Corpus anchor |
|---|---|---|

| **B-2** | For an artifact-only fixture with no `raw_input` field: are the artifact octets the input segment under `raw_input_encoding = identity`, or does §6.3's FIXTURE GAP clause apply and the handoff be refused with `HANDOFF_REJECTED` / `ENCODING_INVALID`? | Option | What it means | What follows |

| **B-II** | `input_segment_sha256` is **receiver-recomputed**, as §7.1's comment says | B-VAL-014 cannot catch BNC-1; §15.3's control mapping needs amending, or BNC-1 is bound to B-VAL-012 alone; B-VAL-014's content reduces to binding the receipt to the segment the receiver holds |

### 5.4 Effect on PRE-01 — none requiring reopening

BC-02.1 v2 marks both operands `RECEIVER_RECOMPUTED`, i.e. it implements B-II.
Its `DigestSource` domain already contains `EXTERNALLY_SUPPLIED`, which can

redesign, without renaming `receipt_digest`, `receipt_digest_source`,
`input_segment_sha256` or `input_segment_sha256_source`, and without collapsing
the two operand paths.** PRE-01 is not reopened by this package, and B's
