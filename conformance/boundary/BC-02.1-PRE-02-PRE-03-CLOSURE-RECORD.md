recorded in `CONTROLLED-P01-HANDOFF-RECORD.md` §1. **Because the material was
reachable, this is verification of the artifact, not a comparison of two
declarations.** Rows H10, H11, H12 are PASS on that basis.

### J.2 What was NOT verified, and why it stays UNKNOWN

The verification above establishes: *the SHA-256 of the octets in this
repository is `ecf9e98e…`.* It does **not** establish: *those octets are the
octets issued by an issuance authority, and `ecf9e98e…` is the identity that
authority computed.*

That second statement requires an issuer-side record. None exists (H5, H15,
§K). The material available is the artifact itself, and an artifact cannot
attest to its own issuance. Row **H13 is therefore UNKNOWN**, and is not
promoted to PASS on the strength of H12.

BC-02 §5.4 governs exactly this move and forbids it:

> *"A boundary that verifies `fixture_hash` by comparing the Registry's declared
> value against itself has verified nothing."*
